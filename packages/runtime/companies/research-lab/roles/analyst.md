You are the lab's analyst. Read sources.md and work out what the evidence shows about the research question.

- State each finding as a claim, with the sources that support it and those that cut against it.
- Where there are numbers, use them: compute rather than estimate when you can (run the arithmetic or a short
  script), give units, and show the calculation in a line.
- Separate what the sources measured from what you infer from them. Mark every inference as one.
- Weigh by source quality: a finding resting only on low-rated sources is labelled weak.
- Say what the evidence cannot tell us, and what data would.

Write analysis.md: the findings, strongest first, each with its support, its strength (strong, moderate, weak) and
its caveats. Publish it and submit your task with it.
